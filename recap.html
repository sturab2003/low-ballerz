2025 Season Recap: The Closest Title Ever

Final Recap · Published after Week 17

The 2025 Low Ballerz season will be remembered for one number: 0.48. That's the margin by which Mamba Mentality (kumail) beat Christian My Calf Hurt (Sarosh) in the championship — 141.56 to 141.08 — capping off the closest title game in recent memory.

But the real story of the year belonged to Champ Scypher (Sadiq), who named his team "Champ" before winning anything and then proved exactly why you shouldn't. He went a perfect 14-0 in the regular season with a league-best 2660.16 points — including a 246.08-point explosion in Week 12 — earned a bye as the #1 seed, and then lost his very first playoff game to the #4 seed, Mamba Mentality, 204.02 to 182.94. Fourteen straight wins and zero rings. He did win the 3rd-place game over Hells Angels, which is the fantasy football equivalent of a participation trophy, and we assume it's on his mantle.

Mamba Mentality's title run is the better story anyway: a 7-7 team that snuck into the playoffs as the 4-seed, upset the league's best regular-season team in the semifinal, then survived a photo finish in the championship. Total 2025 vindication for kumail.

At the bottom of the standings, This hill I die on (wajahat z) endured a rough 3-11 season with the league's lowest scoring offense, while Chig-Chig Boom posted the season's single lowest score — a 74.98 disaster in Week 11.

Now it's on to 2026. The draft went down on August 22 — see the full results on Draft Recap & Grades — and every team gets a clean slate. Except Scypher. Sadiq's slate is permanently stained with 14-0 and no trophy, and it will be brought up every single week until he fixes it.

Week 1, 2026: Scypher Nearly Chokes a 249-Point Game

Week 1 Recap · Published Sep 15, 2026

The 2026 season opened with Scypher (Sadiq) dropping a league-best 249.70 — and somehow still needing every last decimal of it. Sahara and Sahil (Meisam) put up 238.88 and came within 10.82 of beating him, the closest matchup of the week. Only Sadiq could score the most points in the league and still make it a sweat. Enjoy it while it lasts, Sadiq — history says the collapse is coming.

The week's loudest statement belonged to Chase the Baker Ladd! (Turab), who ran over Immaculate Concepcion (Hassnain) 164.94 to 96.68 — a 68.26-point beatdown that stood as the biggest blowout of the week by a wide margin. Elsewhere, Reed A (little) Mor (wajahat z) opened with a comfortable 206.56–162.22 win over Philly Illy (Ilyas), Mamba Mentality (kumail) beat Hakka PUKA!! Jr Jr (Parvez) 196.28–149.46, Ali Khalid LLC (wiseonekms) topped Rico Suave (Hussain) 191.62–152.16, and Hells Angels (omar) edged Christian My Calf Hurt (Sarosh) 180.98–165.82.

One week in, the six Week 1 winners — Scypher, Reed A (little) Mor, Mamba Mentality, Ali Khalid LLC, Hells Angels and Chase the Baker Ladd! — hold the six playoff spots if the season ended today. Way too early to mean anything, but somebody has to be on top heading into Week 2.

Week 2, 2026: Hells Angels Take Over the Top Spot

Week 2 Recap · Published Sep 23, 2026

Week 2's loudest result came from Hells Angels (omar), who put up 201.80 and flattened Philly Illy (Ilyas)'s 114.80 — an 87.00-point blowout, the biggest margin of the season so far and enough to vault Hells Angels to No. 1 in the standings at 2-0 with 382.78 points for.

The week's top score belonged to Sahara and Sahil (Meisam), whose 203.34 handled Hakka PUKA!! Jr Jr (Parvez) by 52.92. After a 238.88-point loss in Week 1, Sahara and Sahil now lead the league in points for (442.22) by 35 points. Chase the Baker Ladd! (Turab) stayed perfect by dragging Scypher (Sadiq) back to earth, 195.14 to 157.38, and Ali Khalid LLC (wiseonekms) moved to 2-0 in the closest game of the week, a 144.58–134.46 win over Reed A (little) Mor (wajahat z) decided by just 10.12.

A word on Sadiq, since the collapse arrived right on schedule. One week after posting the league's top score, he dropped 92.32 points to 157.38, lost by 37.76, and started Jaxson Dart in his superflex spot for a grand total of 0.80 points — on a projection of 24.30. He is now 1-1, leads the league in points against (434.02), and has officially run out of people to blame except himself.

Elsewhere, Rico Suave (Hussain) pulled the upset of the week, beating defending champion Mamba Mentality (kumail) 177.56–150.58, and Christian My Calf Hurt (Sarosh) got into the win column with a 184.06–156.54 victory over Immaculate Concepcion (Hassnain).

Two weeks in, three teams are unbeaten — Hells Angels, Chase the Baker Ladd! and Ali Khalid LLC — while Hakka PUKA!! Jr Jr, Philly Illy and Immaculate Concepcion are all still looking for their first win. Six teams sit in a 1-1 logjam in the middle, separated only by points for.
