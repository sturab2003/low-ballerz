Heads Up: Scoring Changes for 2026

The commissioner made scoring changes on August 16, 2026 (check your email). The headline change: TE Premium is now on — tight ends score 1.5 pts per reception instead of the usual 1 pt. There were also a few defensive scoring tweaks, most notably sacks now worth 1.5 pts (up from 1). The full Scoring Settings table below reflects the current, up-to-date rules.

Keeper & Trade Rules Effective 2027

Voted on by the league in 2026. These don't change anything about the upcoming 2026 draft — that's still 2 keepers per team under the existing rules. They kick in starting with the 2027 season. See the Keepers page for how this affects each team's current keepers.

Keeper Count3 keepers per team, up from 2 — effective the 2027 draft.
Max Keeper YearsA player can be kept for a maximum of 2 years. Starting in 2027, any player already kept 2+ years becomes a free agent.
Keeper CostRound drafted minus 1 round — effective 2027.
2nd-Year Keeper RestrictionsA 2nd-year keeper can't be kept again or traded for next-season purposes. It can only be traded in-season.
In-Season Trade CutoffNo in-season trades are allowed after the last week of the regular season.
Post-Season Trade WindowAfter the regular season ends, only keeper trades and draft pick trades are allowed.
Draft Pick TradingDraft picks can't be traded for in-season purposes — only for keepers or other draft picks, for next season.
Keeper Year Transition1st-year keepers become 2nd-year keepers after the last week of the regular season's Monday Night Football game.
Keeper Deadline ChangesOnce the keeper deadline passes, changes are only allowed if a keeper gets injured. No changes are allowed within 2 hours of the draft.
